# Quran Tracker v10.5.6 — Madani Page Skin & Opening Pages

## Scope
Visual refinement of the Teacher Mushaf only. No student/session schema or Supabase changes.

## What changed
- New scalable Madani-inspired frame with green, rose and restrained gold details.
- Local ornamental corner and Surah-title SVG assets, precached for Offline use.
- Surah-title cartouche appears on pages that begin a Surah.
- Page 1 has a dedicated Al-Fatiha ceremonial layout.
- Page 2 has a dedicated Al-Baqarah opening layout.
- Header/footer controls were tightened to maximize Quran page space on phones.
- Night mode keeps the page legible on a warm dim parchment surface.

## Safety
- Schema: 12 (unchanged)
- SQL migration: none
- Student/session data: unchanged
- Supabase/auth/sync: unchanged
- Existing 604-page Offline pack remains compatible.
